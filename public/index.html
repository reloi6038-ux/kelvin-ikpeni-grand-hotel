Welcome to The Kelvin Ikpeni Grand

A Landmark of Refined Hospitality

Rising above the vibrant skyline of Victoria Island, The Kelvin Ikpeni Grand Hotel is a sanctuary of five-star comfort, architectural beauty, and warm Nigerian hospitality.

From the marble-clad lobby to the skyline-view suites, from our award-winning kitchen to our serene spa, every corner is designed to elevate your experience.
